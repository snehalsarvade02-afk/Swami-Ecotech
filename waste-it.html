How We Recycle IT & Telecommunication

IT and telecommunication equipment form the backbone of modern business, but they also contain highly sensitive data and valuable precious metals like gold, silver, and palladium.

We specialize in the end-to-end decommissioning of IT assets. Our process ensures that all data is irrevocably destroyed before the hardware is broken down and sorted for material recovery.

  • Secure on-site or off-site data wiping and physical shredding.
  • Maximum recovery of precious and semi-precious metals.
  • Complete transparency with Form 6 documentation and video evidence.
