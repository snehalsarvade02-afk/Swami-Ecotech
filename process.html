What We Do

A transparent, secure, and environmentally sound 6-step recycling process.

1

Collection & Transportation

We deploy secure logistics via our eco-friendly fleet to collect e-waste directly from your premises. We ensure EPR-compliant documentation right from the handover.

2

E-Waste Categorization

Assets are thoroughly audited and sorted at our facility. We classify equipment into reusable, repairable, and recyclable categories to maximize ITAD asset value.

3

Secure Data Destruction

Protecting your data is paramount. We utilize internationally certified software (like Blancco) for logical wiping, and physical shredders for complete hardware destruction.

4

Recycling & Component Extraction

Using advanced automated machinery, we safely extract precious metals (gold, silver, copper) and sort plastics, glass, and base metals for the manufacturing supply chain.

5

Hazardous Waste Treatment

Toxic elements such as lead, mercury, and cadmium are isolated and treated in strict accordance with CPCB guidelines, guaranteeing a zero-landfill outcome.

6

Compliance & Reporting

You receive detailed ESG reporting, Form 6 documentation, and Green Certificates to bolster your corporate social responsibility profile.
