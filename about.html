About Swami Ecotech

Leading India's transition to a sustainable, circular economy through responsible e-waste recycling and IT Asset Disposal (ITAD).

Who We Are

Swami Ecotech is an industry-leading environmental services company based in India. We specialize in comprehensive Electronic Waste (E-Waste) Management, IT Asset Disposal (ITAD), and Secure Data Destruction.

Our core philosophy is driven by the 5R Principle: Rethink, Reduce, Reuse, Repair, and Recycle. We offer end-to-end B2B solutions for corporations, minimizing their environmental footprint while maximizing resource recovery and ensuring complete data privacy.

  • Experience: Decades of combined expertise in B2B electronics recycling.
  • Global Standards: Strict adherence to international compliance and safety protocols.
  • Zero Landfill: Committed to ensuring no toxic materials end up in landfills.
