Responsible Recycling

State-of-the-art facility ensuring maximum material recovery compliant with environmental norms.

At our advanced facility, we utilize modern extraction and dismantling techniques specifically designed to handle India's diverse e-waste streams. Our zero-landfill policy ensures that every component is processed sustainably.

The Process: Once e-waste reaches our factory, it undergoes manual sorting by our trained personnel, followed by automated shredding and density separation. We successfully recover valuable resources like copper, aluminum, gold, and plastic, reintroducing them into the manufacturing cycle and preventing toxic runoff into our soils.
