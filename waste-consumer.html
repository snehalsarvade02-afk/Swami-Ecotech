How We Recycle Consumer Electronics

Consumer electronics like televisions, microwaves, and audio systems often contain hazardous materials such as lead in CRT glass and chemical refrigerants in older appliances.

Our specialized dismantling lines are designed to safely separate plastics, ferrous metals, and hazardous components. We ensure that zero toxic leakage occurs, protecting both the environment and human health.

  • Safe extraction of hazardous chemicals and heavy metals.
  • Advanced shredding and sorting for plastics and ferrous metals.
  • Convenient doorstep pickup for residential societies and bulk generators.
